How It Works

This application uses Photoplethysmography (PPG) to estimate blood pressure from your fingertip. By analyzing subtle color changes in your skin caused by blood flow, our AI model can predict your systolic and diastolic blood pressure.

To understand how this works, check out this article.

Privacy First

All data processing and AI inference happens completely on your device.

Your video data and measurements are processed locally in your browser and are never sent to any server. Your privacy is guaranteed.

Important Notes

  • Lighting: Use the app in a well-lit environment or ensure the camera flash is on
  • Pressure: Apply gentle but firm pressure with your fingertip
  • Stillness: Keep your hand steady during the entire recording
  • Calibration: For best accuracy, calibrate with a reference device. The app uses a weighted average of your last 5 readings to improve precision over time.
  • Medical Use: This is for informational purposes only, not for medical diagnosis
